03 — Discovery & Research
Understanding the drop.

Research signals

R.01 Interviewed 14 buyers across social commerce use cases — TikTok Shop, Instagram checkout, creator links.
R.02 Mapped 8 merchant onboarding flows — average 3 redirects before a buyer could complete a purchase.
R.03 Audited 6 competitor embed SDKs — all required full page redirect or iframe with auth handoff.
R.04 Quantified drop-off: 70% of clicks from content to product page lost before add-to-cart.
R.05 Creator interviews: friction kills trust — "If I send them away, I lose them."

What users said

"I tap the link, it opens a new tab, I forget what I was doing. That's when I close it."
"Creators send me 8 steps to buy a thing they showed in 3 seconds."
"The moment I leave the app, the intent is gone."

The insight

Buyers don't abandon because they stop wanting the product. They abandon because the infrastructure makes them work to buy it.

04 — User Flow
Discover. Decide. Done.

Five steps, no redirect. The entire purchase happens inside the surface where the buyer found the product.

01
DISCOVER
Buyer sees product in a creator post, editorial page, or social feed.
02
NUDGE
Embedded checkout widget surfaces inline — no redirect triggered.
03
SELECT
Buyer picks size/variant, reviews price and shipping — stays in context.
04
CONFIRM
Payment via saved method or one-tap. Auth handled by the embed layer.
05
DONE
Order confirmed inline. Buyer stays where they were. Intent preserved.

06 — Requirements
What had to be true.
REQ.01
Zero-redirect checkout
The entire purchase flow must complete inside the host surface. No new tab, no external redirect, no loss of context.
REQ.02
Single embed tag
Merchants integrate via one script tag. No complex SDK install, no backend dependency for the host publisher.
REQ.03
Auth handled by the layer
Payment auth, session management, and saved-method lookup happen inside the embed — invisible to the buyer.
REQ.04
Merchant analytics dashboard
Conversion rate, abandonment by surface, revenue attribution per embed, and cohort comparison — all owned by the merchant.
REQ.05
Variant + inventory sync
Real-time inventory and variant state pulled from the merchant's existing catalog. No double-entry for merchants.
REQ.06
Sub-2-second load
Widget renders in under 2 seconds at p95. Any slower and the intent window closes. Non-negotiable.
REQ.07
Fallback path
If embed fails to load, buyer is redirected to the product page — never stranded. Fallback is automatic and silent.
REQ.08
Publisher revenue share hooks
Attribution API exposes conversion events so publishers and creators can claim commission without manual reconciliation.

08 — Learnings
What this taught me.
L.01 The product wasn't the checkout widget — it was eliminating the redirect. Defining the right problem first made every requirement obvious.
L.02 Merchants care about attribution, not just conversion. Revenue share hooks were an afterthought in the brief and became the most requested feature in interviews.
L.03 The fallback path mattered more than the happy path. Engineers trusted the spec once they saw edge cases were handled, not hand-waved.
L.04 A 0-to-1 product needs a simpler PRD, not a more complete one. Scope discipline at week 2 saved four weeks of scope-creep negotiation at week 6.
Intent is perishable. Nudge keeps it alive.
