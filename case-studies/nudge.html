Nudge · Concept Case Study

Checkout at the moment of intent.

Nudge is a concept case study exploring how checkout can happen inside the buying moment instead of after a redirect. The goal was to reduce handoff friction and keep the buyer in context.

Read PRD Read Case Study
Problem · Why I explored this

High-intent buyers often discover products inside blogs, reviews, and recommendation pages, but checkout usually happens after a redirect. That handoff breaks context, cools down intent, and adds friction exactly when the buyer is most ready to act. Nudge explores whether checkout can happen inside the buying moment itself — without forcing the user to leave the content surface.
