RTO Guard

Post-purchase risk dashboard for detecting likely-to-fail e-commerce deliveries before they become returns.

Read PRD Read Case Study
Problem · Why I explored this

D2C and e-commerce brands often lose margin after an order has already started failing — repeated delivery attempts, unreachable customers, address issues, COD risk, and delayed courier updates all show up too late. Ops teams usually work from scattered dashboards, manual follow-ups, and reactive reports.

RTO Guard explores how a post-purchase risk dashboard could help operations teams identify risky orders earlier, understand why they are at risk, and take action before the order becomes RTO.

Product Flow
Step 01

Dashboard overview

Ops manager gets a quick view of risky orders, exceptions, and delivery health.

    Problem

    Ops teams lack an early-warning system for orders likely to fail delivery.

    Persona

    D2C operations manager handling 500–5,000 orders per day.

    Goals
    • Surface risky orders early
    • Explain why each order is risky
    • Reduce manual follow-up effort
    • Help teams prioritize action
    • Track RTO patterns and courier performance
    Product Thinking

    This concept focuses on turning scattered delivery signals into a focused risk workflow. Instead of showing only shipment status, the dashboard highlights risk status, reason codes, priority, and suggested next action.

    Design Decisions
    • Risk-first dashboard instead of status-only tracking
    • Clear risk categories: On Track, At Risk, Exception
    • Order-level drilldown for root-cause visibility
    • Action-oriented layout for ops teams
    • Simple trend and courier insights for monitoring
    Outcome · What this demonstrates

    This case study demonstrates business analysis, dashboard thinking, workflow mapping, risk-prioritization logic, and product storytelling for a post-purchase operations problem.
