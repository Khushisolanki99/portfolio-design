§ 01 Problem

Too late
is the
default.

D2C and e-commerce brands often lose margin after an order has already started failing — repeated delivery attempts, unreachable customers, address issues, COD risk, and delayed courier updates all show up too late. Ops teams work from scattered dashboards, manual follow-ups, and reactive reports.

RTO Guard explores how a post-purchase risk dashboard could help operations teams identify risky orders earlier, understand why they are at risk, and take action before the order becomes RTO.

Product Flow
Step 01

Dashboard overview

Ops manager gets a quick view of risky orders, exceptions, and delivery health.

    Overview

    Ops teams lack an early-warning system
    for orders likely to fail delivery.

    RTO Guard turns scattered delivery signals into a focused risk workflow. Instead of showing only shipment status, the dashboard surfaces risk status, reason codes, priority, and the suggested next action — before ops gets a call from an angry customer.

    01 Persona

    D2C operations manager handling 500–5,000 orders per day. Responsible for delivery performance, exception management, and reducing return-to-origin rates across multiple couriers and regions.

    02 Goals
    • Surface risky orders early, before failure locks in
    • Explain why each order is at risk
    • Reduce manual follow-up and triage effort
    • Help teams prioritize which orders to act on first
    • Track RTO patterns and courier performance over time
    Product Thinking

    Risk-first, not status-first.

    Most delivery dashboards answer "where is my order?" RTO Guard answers "which orders are about to fail, and what should I do about them?" That shift — from status reporting to risk prioritization — is the core product decision. Clear risk categories, order-level drilldown, and action-oriented layout are all downstream of that framing.

    03 Design Decisions
    • Risk-first dashboard instead of status-only tracking
    • Clear risk tiers: On Track, At Risk, Exception
    • Order-level drilldown for root-cause visibility
    • Action panel surfaces the suggested next step
    • Trend and courier view for pattern monitoring
    04 What this demonstrates

    Business analysis, dashboard thinking, workflow mapping, risk-prioritization logic, and product storytelling for a post-purchase operations problem.
