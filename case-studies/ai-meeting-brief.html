§ 01 Problem

Context
lost in
transit.

Meetings often happen without enough context. Product managers, business analysts, and cross-functional teams spend time jumping between calendars, notes, documents, and previous discussions just to understand what needs attention before the call even starts.

After the meeting, decisions and action items are scattered or forgotten. AI Meeting Brief explores how an AI-assisted workspace could prepare users before a meeting, surface relevant context, capture key decisions, and convert discussions into clear follow-up actions.

    Overview

    Teams lose time preparing for meetings and fail to convert
    discussions into clear decisions and actions.

    AI Meeting Brief connects pre-meeting preparation with post-meeting execution. Instead of treating meetings as isolated calendar events, the workspace turns them into structured workflows — with context, decisions, and follow-up accountability all in one place.

    01 Persona

    Product manager or business analyst working with cross-functional stakeholders. Spends significant time on meeting prep and often leaves meetings with unclear ownership of next steps.

    02 Goals
    • Reduce meeting preparation time significantly
    • Surface relevant context before the discussion starts
    • Make stakeholder expectations easier to understand
    • Capture decisions clearly and with attribution
    • Convert follow-ups into trackable action items
    Product Thinking

    Meetings aren't events. They're workflows.

    The concept focuses on connecting pre-meeting preparation with post-meeting execution. Instead of treating meetings as isolated calendar blocks, the workspace turns them into structured workflows: the AI agent pulls company context and news, generates a focused brief, captures decisions in real time, and converts action items into trackable outputs. The calendar is just the entry point.

    03 Design Decisions
    • Meeting-first dashboard — calendar is the anchor
    • AI brief generated from multiple context sources
    • Clear agenda and risk sections in every brief
    • Post-meeting summary with explicit decision capture
    • Action tracker connected to meeting outcomes
    04 What this demonstrates

    AI product thinking, workflow design, business analysis, meeting operations, decision tracking, and product storytelling.
