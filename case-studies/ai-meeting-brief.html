AI Meeting Brief

An AI workspace that helps teams prepare for meetings, understand context, capture decisions, and turn discussions into follow-up actions.

Read PRD Read Case Study
Problem · Why I explored this

Meetings often happen without enough context. Product managers, business analysts, and cross-functional teams spend time jumping between calendars, notes, documents, and previous discussions just to understand what needs attention. After the meeting, decisions and action items are often scattered or forgotten.

AI Meeting Brief explores how an AI-assisted workspace could prepare users before a meeting, surface relevant context, capture key decisions, and convert discussions into clear follow-up actions.

Dashboard

    Problem

    Teams lose time preparing for meetings and often fail to convert meeting discussions into clear decisions and actions.

    Persona

    Product manager or business analyst working with cross-functional stakeholders.

    Goals
    • Reduce meeting preparation time
    • Surface relevant context before discussion
    • Make stakeholder expectations easier to understand
    • Capture decisions clearly
    • Convert follow-ups into trackable action items
    Product thinking

    This concept focuses on connecting pre-meeting preparation with post-meeting execution. Instead of treating meetings as isolated calendar events, the workspace turns them into structured workflows with context, decisions, and follow-up accountability.

    Design decisions
    • Meeting-first dashboard
    • AI-generated brief from multiple inputs
    • Clear agenda and risk sections
    • Post-meeting summary with decision capture
    • Action tracker connected to meeting outcomes
    Outcome · What this demonstrates

    This case study demonstrates AI product thinking, workflow design, business analysis, meeting operations, decision tracking, and product storytelling.
